About Me

I'm a multidisciplinary designer specializing in visual storytelling. My general specialties include designing, writing, and user testing interactive and multimedia projects.

In 2014, I graduated from Columbia College Chicago with a degree in Game Design, but spent nearly the next decade in educational publishing, specializing in digital asset management and design.

In 2022, I made a career change and worked as a QA Analyst at Netherrealm Studios, primarily contributing to the production of Mortal Kombat 1, which was released in 2023.

I developed an interest in UX design to complement my technical and creative skills and have been working towards specializing in interaction design for games.

I am now seeking new opportunities in the game development industry, while also remaining open to the broader tech industry and adjacent avenues to apply and grow my skill set.
