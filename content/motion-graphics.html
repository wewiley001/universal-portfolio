VFX / Motion Graphics

I have over a decade of experience in multimedia production, often making VFX for video as a freelance editor. Below are videos showcasing my work with After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Animated Motion Graphics YouTube Intro

Using green screen videos of anime characters and PNG foreground/background assets, this animated intro sequence was designed to be minimalistic — in the style of James Bond films, Archer, etc. Made in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and After Effects.

Night City Apartment Virtual Background

A virtual background for video calls and live streaming. The room is a real 2D image with windows removed and color-tinted for a nighttime setting. A Cyberpunk 2077 skyline video was composited behind the 2D image on a 3D composition. Made in Photoshop and After Effects.

Fake 3D Motion Graphic Video

An intro screen created by placing a 2D image on a 3D composition and moving a virtual camera through a pseudo-3D space. Made with Photoshop and After Effects.

Motion Graphic End Screen Video

End screen motion graphics using various 2D images on a 3D composition. All moving and static elements — foregrounds, clouds, skyline — are 2D images. Made with Photoshop and After Effects.
