Space Assault

Written By W.E. Wiley
EXT. Starbase Excelsior — Orbit of Planet Aris

The GUNMARE, a large one-person Starfighter, is attacking the massive space station Excelsior, leaving it heavily damaged with hull breaches and flames covering most of it. The station’s weapons hopelessly fire at it, unable to catch it.

INT. Command Deck: Starbase Excelsior — Space

Red lights are flashing and alarms are going off as command officers frantically work at their various stations. General Kamad sits in his command chair.

General Kamad

How the HELL did this happen!!!

Operations Officer

They just dropped out of hyperspace and began firing on the station. They’ve targeted our primary engines and shield generator.

General Kamad

How many ships?

Operations Officer

One, Sir.

General Kamad

One ship! Are you telling me that the most powerful military station in Valinor Space is being defeated by one ship?

Operations Officer

It looks like a fighter but matches no known ships in the Ascendancy. I don’t detect any life signs onboard. It might be running an automated attack program.

General Kamad

If no one’s onboard, why can’t we shoot them down?

Tactical Officer

Sir, I can’t get a lock on them. Something is disrupting our sensor array. Someone’s using it as a distraction, most likely.

Communications Officer

Sir, we’re being hailed. It’s a holo feed.

General Kamad

Display.

A holodais in front of the bridge projects a hologram of the Archangel in body armor covered with a heavy cloak and full helm.

Archangel (Hologram)

General Kamad, I presume.

General Kamad

Are you responsible for this? I will have your head on a platter when I get my hands—

Archangel (Hologram)

You rule over your Valinor Dominion quite poorly. Usually, I try not to interfere with the political struggles of other galactic sovereignties, but that business on New Charon changed all that.

General Kamad

The rebels who opposed me were operating in that world. I had every right to kill them.

Archangel (Hologram)

By ordering an orbital bombardment on the planet, with nuclear warheads, no less. You could have killed millions of lives in that world with plasma cannons or a Nova Bomb. But no — you use an outdated weapon to not only destroy the lives of the innocents there but to kill the planet with radiation as a message.

General Kamad

I don’t have to explain myself to you. I will see you die for what you’ve done on this day.

Archangel (Hologram)

No, you won’t.

Communications Officer

(to Kamad) Sir, this transmission is coming from somewhere in the station.

General Kamad

Trace it. (to Archangel) I have more than a thousand Marines on this station. You have no hope of escaping.

Archangel (Hologram)

I’m coming for you, General. If you have a god, look to the horizon and pray to him now. Dawn is breaking.

The transmission cuts off and the holo image of the Archangel disappears.

Communications Officer

Sir, I’ve found the source of the transmission. It’s coming from a COM terminal in the lower part of the central cluster. He’s right below us, sir.

General Kamad

Send an attachment of Marines to his location. I want him alive.

Tactical Officer

Yes sir.

INT. Command Access: Starbase Excelsior — Space

The scene is chaotic as Royal Marines mobilize into a defensive position around a bulkhead, aiming their blasters down the corridor. Archangel walks toward them from the opposite end with two heavy blasters.

Marine Captain

Fire!!!

The Marines fire, but the plasma bolts refract around Archangel via a transparent shield, leaving him unharmed. He doesn’t stop his approach.

Marine #1

The shots aren’t even touching him.

Marine #2

This isn’t even possible! How can he be doing this?

Marine Captain

Don’t let up. He has to break sometime.

Archangel charges, dodging bolts and attacking with a flurry of punches and kicks before releasing a shockwave that sends men flying in all directions. He then unleashes a chain of lightning that electrocutes several Marines to death. More than half the group is killed.

Marine Captain

Shit, he’s a Void User. Fall Back!

INT. Command Deck: Starbase Excelsior — Space

On the command deck, alarms continue and explosions shake the station. The Operations Officer combs through holographic records on her console until she finds a match for the GUNMARE.

Operations Officer

Sir, you’ll want to know about this.

General Kamad

What?!

Operations Officer

It’s about that starfighter. I widened the search parameters to find a match. That ship was seen twenty years ago during the Raider Wars in the Andromeda Galaxy. It’s also appeared in several isolated incidents throughout the Ascendency for the past eighty years. And they all had one thing in common.

General Kamad

Which was?

Operations Officer

The Archangel.

Everyone on the bridge freezes at the mention of his name, a look of dread falling over them all.

General Kamad

“The immortal warrior who slays armies with sword and spell.” Cheap tricks. False legends…

Science Officer

I’ve heard stories of him being able to create electrical storms from his fingertips.

Communications Officer

I heard he can pull starships out of the sky with his mind.

Operations Officer

If he is even a fifth of what the stories say of him, how can we stop a person like that?

General Kamad

Enough. I will not have your minds disturbed by some myth.

Tactical Officer

I fought in the Raider Wars, sir. They called him the Swordsman of Death. I may not have fought by his side, but the results of what he’s done with the power he possesses are all too real. The Kinkari Crusaders have been hunting him for centuries. If this man is truly him, then you would be a fool to—

General Kamad draws his blaster and fires a plasma bolt into the Tactical Officer’s chest, killing him. Everyone freezes at the sight.

General Kamad

He is but one man! And any man can die. I will make you see this. And if I hear talk of these foolish stories anymore, you will go the way of our late tactical officer over there. Do I make myself clear?!

INT. Command Access: Starbase Excelsior — Space

The Marines scramble back from the passageway. The Captain moves to a control panel and seals the bulkhead. Before it fully shuts, he tosses a thermal charge through the gap. The resulting blast reverberates through the sealed door.

Marine #3

Is he dead?

Marine #4

He has to be. That was a thermal charge. No one can survive inside the blast radius, let alone one that blows up in your face.

The Marine Captain’s headset activates. He opens the holographic computer on his wrist and answers the call from General Kamad.

General Kamad

Captain. Status report.

Marine Captain

Yes, Your Excellency. I’ve lost nearly all of my Marines. This man — he was a Will-User. We stopped the Archangel in the main corridor with a thermal charge, but we are unable to confirm his death at this time.

General Kamad

Don’t bother. Your stunt managed to rupture that part of the deck. He was probably either killed in the explosions or vented into space.

Marine Captain

I’m sorry for the damage, Sir —

A massive force slams against the closed bulkhead, startling everyone.

Marine #1

That’s not possible.

The door gradually begins to glow red and yellow from the heat.

General Kamad

Captain? What is happening? Answer me!

A massive beam of blue energy tears through the door, enveloping all the Marines.

INT. Command Deck: Starbase Excelsior — Space

General Kamad and the rest of the bridge crew hear the Marines’ screams — then sudden silence.

General Kamad

Captain? What happened? Captain!?!

Communications Officer

Sir, sensors detected that eleven life signs in the Command Access have just disappeared. Only one remains.

General Kamad

I want to see a security vid of what just happened!

On the massive viewscreen, a recording plays: a grenade is thrown at Archangel’s feet through a closing bulkhead door. He throws up his hand as it explodes. When the image clears, everything in the room is damaged — but Archangel stands alive, surrounded by a transparent shield. He takes a martial stance, focuses energy into his hand, and releases a destructive beam toward the door. The last image shows the Marines being enveloped in light before the feed cuts out.

General Kamad

That’s not possible. That’s…not…POSSIBLE!

The door to the bridge is blasted open with an explosion, knocking several security guards out. The Archangel steps through. General Kamad raises his sidearm, but Archangel waves it away telekinetically, sending it skidding across the bridge. He approaches as the officers stand frozen.

Archangel

If this man is worth dying for, then stay and fight me. Otherwise, evacuate the station. Now.

The bridge officers exchange weary glances — then turn and flee.

General Kamad

Wait! I am your general! Your Ruler! Come back, you cowards!

Archangel

A general can be measured by the quality of his enemies. But a leader can be defined by the loyalty of his followers. And you are a poor leader indeed.

General Kamad

And what about the lives of the people you killed on this starbase? So many innocents were slain in battle just to get to me. You are to blame for that.

Archangel

Don’t you DARE lecture me on the nature of war!

The Archangel seizes General Kamad by the scruff of his neck and hurls him across the bridge into a workstation panel. Kamad crumples to the floor as sparks fly.

Archangel

I have fought and shed blood long before your rise to power over the Dominion. Long before the rise of the Dominion itself! Your actions at New Charon brought all I’ve wrought upon your head!

General Kamad

I don’t care what the stories say about you. I have friends. I have allies. Immortal or not, you will answer for what you’ve done here.

Archangel

As will you.

The Archangel lifts General Kamad telekinetically and crushes his torso, splattering blood across the bridge. He drops the corpse and walks to the console, hacking into the system.

Station Computer (over ship coms)

Auto Self-Destruct Protocols initiated. T-Minus 2 standard minutes and counting.

Archangel opens his Hop-COM — a holographic wrist-mounted computer — and inputs a sequence of commands.

EXT. Starbase Excelsior — Orbit of Planet Aris

The GUNMARE breaks off its assault and crosses to the far side of the starbase, hovering before the bridge viewport. It fires a plasma blast, shattering the glass with an explosion. Archangel is sucked into the vacuum of space, floats to the GUNMARE — and dematerializes.

INT. Gunmare: Cockpit — Space

The Archangel materializes inside the cockpit, drops into the pilot’s chair, and grips the controls.

EXT. Starbase Excelsior — Orbit of Planet Aris

The GUNMARE moves away from the station and leaps into hyperspace as the starbase explodes behind it.

INT. Gunmare: Cockpit — Hyperspace

The Archangel removes his helmet, revealing a young man who appears to be in his late twenties, with oddly luminescent blue eyes. He opens a channel on the communications array and waits.

Shanks (over com)

This is the Midsummer Night’s Dream. Identify yourself.

Archangel

As dreary as always, Shanks.

Diana (over com)

Oh, Archangel. You’re not dead?

Archangel

That’s you, Thiana? You sound disappointed.

Diana (over com)

I had a pool going that you wouldn’t come back.

Archangel

You bet against me?

Diana (over com)

Of course not. I know you better than that…Commander.

Archangel

Anyway, I’m heading to the rendezvous point now. ETA seven minutes.

Diana (over com)

Very well. We’ll be ready. Don’t keep us waiting.

EXT. The Midsummer Night’s Dream — Orbit of Lambda Hyper Gate

The GUNMARE exits hyperspace and flies toward a bronze light cruiser — the Midsummer Night’s Dream. The docking bay shields drop and the GUNMARE lands inside.
